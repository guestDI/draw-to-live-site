Common questions

  • Where is the grown-up corner? Press and hold the small person icon at the top right of the home screen for two seconds, then answer the sum.
  • How do I manage or cancel Magic pictures? In the iPad Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Subscriptions are managed by Apple.
  • I bought Magic pictures on another iPad. Open the grown-up corner and choose Restore.
  • How do I delete everything? Delete the app from the iPad. Monsters, drawings and recordings are stored only on the iPad.
